# Client portal guide

**For:** clients following their project in the portal. No technical knowledge needed.

The client portal is where your project lives: progress, approvals, meetings, messages and
files, in English or Arabic. This guide uses the button and section names you see in the
English portal; the Arabic portal has the same layout, right to left.

## Getting access

There is no sign-up page. You get an account in one of two ways:

- **You accepted a proposal.** Accepting asks you to choose a password; your account and
  your project are created at the same moment, and you are signed in straight away.
- **You received an invitation email.** Open the link and choose a password (and,
  optionally, the name shown on your messages). Invitation links work for **7 days**. If
  yours has expired or was withdrawn, ask for a new one.

Already have an account from an earlier project? The invitation or proposal asks for your
existing password instead, and adds the new project to your account.

Passwords need at least 10 characters. Very long passwords are limited to 72 bytes, which
is about 72 English letters or 36 Arabic letters.

## Signing in

- Go to the portal sign-in page, or use **Client login** at the bottom of
  [michael-zakka.com](https://michael-zakka.com).
- Signing in keeps you signed in on that device for up to 7 days.
- **Forgot your password?** Use the link on the sign-in page. The reset link in the email
  works once, for 60 minutes. Resetting your password signs you out on every other device.
- After too many failed attempts in a short time, sign-in pauses for a few minutes. Wait
  and try again, or reset your password.

## Your projects

After signing in you see **Your projects**: each with its progress, current phase and
estimated completion. Badges point out what needs you:

- **Needs your approval**: a milestone is waiting for you to approve it.
- **Your feedback is needed**: the project is complete and waiting for your sign-off.
- **New messages**: unread messages, with a count.

Finished projects that were archived are listed separately under **Archived**.

## Inside a project

Each project has six sections: **Overview, Milestones, Meetings, Messages, Files** and
**Activity**.

### Overview

The status, current phase, estimated completion and the progress bar, plus your current
milestone, the next meeting, the latest message and the number of files shared.

The progress bar is weighted by how long each milestone is expected to take, so a
three-week phase moves it further than a two-day one. A milestone in progress counts
partly, by its tasks, but the bar only reaches 100% when every milestone is approved.

### Milestones and tasks

Milestones are the phases of the project, in order, each with its due date. Under each
milestone you see the tasks that were shared with you.

**Approving a milestone.** When a milestone is ready you get an email, and the milestone
shows **Ready for your approval**, sometimes with a note on what to check and a preview
link. Then:

- **Approve** confirms the work in this milestone is complete. It is marked done.
- **Request changes** asks you what should change. The milestone goes back into
  progress, and a **Changes requested** thread opens under it, where you and Michael can
  reply to each other until the next version is ready for approval.

Any client on the project can answer. Everyone sees who approved or asked for changes,
and when.

**Commenting on a task.** Every task shared with you has a comment thread. Ask a
question or give feedback there, with files attached if needed; Michael is notified.

### Meetings

- **Propose a meeting** with a topic, a date and time (in your local time), a duration
  (15 minutes to 2 hours), an optional agenda and, optionally, the milestone it is about.
- The other side then **Accepts** or **Suggests another time**. Either side can suggest a
  new time or **Cancel**, even after a meeting was confirmed. Whoever acts, the other side
  is emailed.
- Meetings take place on Google Meet. Michael adds the link once the time is set, and you
  are emailed when he does.
- **Add to calendar** downloads the meeting for your calendar app, with its own reminders
  a day and an hour before. You also get a reminder email the day before.

**Meeting notes.** After a meeting, Michael shares notes: a summary and action items, each
for you or for him. You're emailed when they are published. Under the notes you can:

- press **Looks right** to confirm the notes match what was agreed,
- **Add a comment or correction**, and
- tick off the action items that are yours.

If the notes are edited after you confirmed them, the confirmation is cleared so you can
look again.

### Messages

One conversation per project, between you (and anyone else on your side) and Michael.

- Write a message and press **Send**, or Ctrl + Enter. Up to 5000 characters.
- **Attach files**: up to 5 per message. They also appear under Files.
- New messages appear by themselves within about 15 seconds; there is no need to reload.
- Older messages load with **Show earlier messages**.
- You're emailed about new messages, but not once per message: one email covers
  everything that arrived until you read the conversation (or a day passes).

### Files

- **Upload a file**: PDF, images (PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP), Word, Excel, PowerPoint, ZIP, TXT
  or CSV. The upload form shows the size limit (normally 25 MB per file). A file whose
  content doesn't match its extension is refused.
- Images appear as a gallery you can open full screen; other files are listed under
  **Documents**.
- Downloading saves the file to your device; files never open as web pages.

### Activity

A timeline of everything that happened on the project: milestones, approvals, meetings,
messages, files and more, colour-coded by kind (the **Colour key** explains the colours).

## Emails you will receive

All emails are in your portal language and link straight to the right place.

| When | Email |
| --- | --- |
| You're invited to a project | Invitation with a link (valid 7 days) |
| A request of yours was accepted without a proposal | "Request accepted" with an invitation link |
| A proposal is sent to you | The proposal link |
| New messages arrive | One email per batch of unread messages |
| Michael asks you to approve a milestone, or replies to your change request | Approval request / reply |
| A meeting is proposed, rescheduled, confirmed or cancelled, or gets its link | Meeting update (confirmations and cancellations include a calendar file) |
| A confirmed meeting is coming up | Reminder the day before |
| Meeting notes are published | The notes and action items |
| Michael comments on a task you can see | Task comment (batched) |
| The project status changes | Status update |
| The project is complete | Request to confirm and review |
| You asked for a password reset | Reset link (valid 60 minutes) |

## Language and time zone

- Switch between English and العربية with the language switch. Your choice is saved to
  your account and decides the language of **every email** you receive.
- Dates and times are shown in your own time zone, detected from your browser. The
  account page shows which zone is in use.

## Your account

Open your account page to:

- change the **name** shown on your messages, files and approvals,
- change your **password** (this signs you out on your other devices), and
- **Sign out on all other devices** if you were signed in somewhere you no longer use.

Your email address is your sign-in; to change it, ask in a message.

## When the project is complete

When Michael marks the project complete, you're emailed and the project shows **Your
project is complete** with **Confirm & share feedback**. It takes about three minutes:

1. **Is everything delivered as agreed?** Answer **Yes, the project is complete**, or
   **Not yet, something is missing** and say what. Michael is notified and follows up;
   you can change your answer to yes later.
2. **How did it go?** A short review. Two questions are required (overall satisfaction,
   and how easy the portal was to use); the rest is optional: how likely you are to
   recommend Michael (0 to 10), which parts of the portal were hard, ratings of the work
   and the communication, and your own words.

Your answers are **private**: only Michael sees them, and they are never published. A
review can't be changed after you submit it.

## Archived projects

An archived project stays readable: you can still see its history and download its files.
Messages, uploads, new meetings and comments are closed. You can still complete the
sign-off if it is open.

## Common questions

**My invitation link says it expired or was withdrawn.** Ask Michael for a new one (reply
to the invitation email). Each new link replaces the previous one.

**I didn't get the email.** Check your spam folder, then ask Michael: he can resend it or
send you the link directly.

**Why can't I create an account myself?** The portal is invite-only on purpose, so only
people working on a project can get in.

**My emails arrive in the wrong language.** Switch the language in the portal once while
signed in; your emails follow that choice from then on.

**I was signed out.** Sessions last 7 days. Changing or resetting your password, or using
"Sign out on all other devices", also ends sessions elsewhere.

**My upload was refused.** Check the file type and size against the list under Files. If
the type is right, the file may be damaged or renamed from another type.
